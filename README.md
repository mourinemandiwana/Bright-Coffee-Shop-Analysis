Bright-Coffee-Shop-Analysis
This project focuses on analysing the “Bright Coffee Shop Sales” dataset, which contains daily transactional data from a coffee shop. The analysis was conducted to support the new CEO’s goal of increasing revenue and improving overall product performance.

As a Junior Data Analyst, the objective was to extract meaningful insights from historical data and present actionable recommendations to guide strategic decision-making.

- Objective
--The analysis aimed to answer the following key business questions:
--Which products generate the most revenue
--What time of day the store performs best
--Sales trends across products and time intervals
--How to improve overall sales performance through data-driven recommendations
      
- Tools and Technologies
      -Databricks – Data cleaning, transformation, and SQL analysis
     -Excel – Data analysis, pivot tables, and visualisations
     -Miro – Data planning and structuring the analytical approach
     -Gantt Chart – Project planning and timeline tracking
  
- Key Insights
     -Revenue is heavily driven by core beverage categories, particularly Coffee and Tea
     -Sales are significantly higher during weekdays compared to weekends
     -A consistent upward sales trend was observed, with peaks during colder months (e.g. June)
     -Several product categories underperform, indicating opportunities for optimisationrpose
  
- Recommendations
    -Promote high-performing products through upselling and combo deals
    -Introduce Buy 1 Get 1 Free promotions to boost sales during low-demand periods
    -Leverage seasonal demand (e.g. colder months) to increase beverage sales
    -Improve performance of low-selling products through bundling and discounts
    -Tailor strategies based on store-specific customer preferences
  
- Business Impact
- This analysis provides actionable insights that can help Bright Coffee Shop:

   -Increase revenue
   -Improve product performance
   -Enhance customer experience
   -Make informed, data-driven decisions
